# **Customer Churn Analysis**

## **Project Overview**

This project analyzes customer data to identify key factors contributing to customer churn, examine customer behavior, and generate insights to support customer retention strategies.

## **Project Details**

- **Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Google Looker Studio
- **Dataset:** Customer Churn Dataset
- **Total Records:** 5,000
- **Total Customers:** 5,000

## **Objectives**

- Identify the overall customer churn rate.
- Analyze churn patterns across subscription types and contract lengths.
- Examine the relationship between support calls and customer churn.
- Evaluate how payment delays relate to customer churn.
- Identify high-risk customer segments to support retention strategies.

## **Analysis Process**

### **1. Data Cleaning & Preparation**

Performed data cleaning, validation, and feature creation to improve dataset consistency and prepare the data for analysis.

**Key Activities:**

- Checked and validated dataset structure.
- Cleaned and standardized customer records.
- Created additional analytical features:
  - Customer Status
  - Risk Category
  - Payment Delay Group
  - Usage Frequency Group

### **2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

Used Excel Pivot Tables to explore customer churn patterns and examine relationships between churn and key customer attributes.

**Business Questions:**

- How does churn differ across contract lengths and subscription types?
- How do support calls relate to customer churn?
- How does payment behavior influence customer churn?
- How does usage frequency differ across customer groups?

**Key Findings:**

- Monthly contracts recorded the highest churn rate at 51.48%.
- Customers making more than five support calls showed churn rates above 60%.
- Customers with high payment delays recorded the highest churn rate at 70.09%.

### **3. Customer Churn Dashboard**

Developed an interactive dashboard using Google Looker Studio to visualize customer churn patterns and compare different customer segments.

**Dashboard Overview:**

- Total Customers: 5,000
- Churned Customers: 2,368
- Overall Churn Rate: 47.36%

**Key Analysis:**

- Customer Churn Distribution
- Churn Rate by Contract Length
- Churn Rate by Subscription Type
- Churn Rate by Support Calls
- Churn Rate by Payment Delay
- Churn Rate by Usage Frequency
- Customer Risk Segmentation

**Interactive Dashboard:**

[**View Customer Churn Dashboard – Google Looker Studio**](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/6509ff6c-c9b0-4e50-a8a6-c8c46e7c92a1)

## **Key Business Recommendations**

- Encourage monthly subscribers to consider longer-term contracts through targeted offers.
- Improve customer support to address recurring issues and reduce churn risk.
- Prioritize customers with high payment delays for proactive retention activities.
- Monitor high-risk customer segments and develop targeted retention strategies.
